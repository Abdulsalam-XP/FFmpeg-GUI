What's New in This Update:

Version 3.0 - the Video Editor grows into a real multi-track editor, the app
gets a second look, and audio in the preview finally behaves.

The editor:

- Real tracks, like an editor: video and audio lanes, linked pairs, drag clips
  along the timeline and between rows, snap to edges (toggleable), reorder
  lanes, delete or mute whole rows.
- Drop extra videos, music files or images straight onto the timeline - a new
  track is created for you. Montage past the end of your clip over black works
  too.
- Per-track volume faders that you can actually hear: every change - boosts
  included - applies to the preview instantly, and both audio tracks of a
  recording (game + microphone) play together correctly.
- The playhead is draggable (grab the line or click anywhere on the strip),
  the timeline scrolls sideways with room past your clip, and zooming with
  Ctrl+wheel is smooth.
- Your work saves properly now: Ctrl+S, a save prompt when you close, and
  saved edits listed in recents as "Saved file:" - reopening one restores the
  whole timeline. Project files live inside the app, not next to your videos.
- Captions, keyframe zooms, fades, cut-list trimming and export all still work
  exactly as before, now on top of the tracks.

The look:

- A second theme: "Petalfall" - an ink-and-white look with falling red petals,
  next to the reworked "Midnight Gold", whose background is now a living
  nebula sky with drifting clouds, twinkling stars and the occasional
  shooting star. Switch between them in Settings with one click.

Also new since 2.0:

- Settings shows the version of ffmpeg, ffprobe and yt-dlp you actually have
  and offers a one-click Update when a newer build is out. Keeping yt-dlp
  current is the single best fix for "YouTube links stopped working".
- The video you pick shows as a card with a thumbnail, resolution, frame
  rate, length and size.
- Recent files on every screen, so you rarely need the file picker at all.

Fixed:

- Cancel works every time, not just for the first job of a session.
- Starting a job while another runs is blocked instead of corrupting both.
- Some recordings played the microphone twice in the preview while the game
  audio stayed silent - the preview now plays every audio stream correctly.
- Updating a tool in Settings no longer keeps offering the same update after
  it already installed.
