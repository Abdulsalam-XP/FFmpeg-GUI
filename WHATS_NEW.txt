What's New in This Update:

Version 3.0.2 - the app now shows you it is working: a loading screen at
launch, a step-by-step tracker for YouTube downloads, and a badge when a tool
needs updating.

New:

- YouTube downloads show the video before you commit to it: a large preview
  image, the title, the channel and the length appear as soon as the link is
  found.
- Every quality option lists its download size (for example
  "Full HD (1080p) - ~128 MB"), so you know what you are getting before you
  press Download. The MP3 panel shows the audio size on the video card.
- A step tracker under the progress bar: Find video, Connect, Download,
  Finish. Each step has its own timer, so a slow start no longer looks like
  a stuck one.
- A glowing "!" appears next to Settings when ffmpeg or yt-dlp has an update
  waiting. It clears when you open Settings; a matching "!" stays beside the
  tool itself until you update it.
- A loading screen at launch: the app icon with a gold ring spinning around
  it, from the moment you start the launcher until the window is ready.
  NOTE: this one lives in Launcher.exe, which the in-app update does not
  replace. To get it, download the new zip from the GitHub releases page.

Improved:

- "Find qualities" no longer freezes the window while it talks to YouTube.
  It runs in the background, and Cancel stops it.
- A failed download now says why (video unavailable, a tool that needs
  updating, and so on) instead of a bare "exit 1". Hover the message for the
  full detail.
- The note above the tool list in Settings is bigger, bold and in the accent
  colour, so it is harder to miss.

Fixed:

- The Full HD, HD, SD and Low qualities could fail with "Requested format is
  not available" on videos where YouTube no longer offers a single-file
  version. Every quality now downloads video and audio separately and joins
  them, the way 2K and above already did.
