What's New in This Update:

Version 2.0 - the tool is now a real app, not a terminal menu.

• Brand new window. No more typing numbers into a black console screen. Every tool
  has its own screen with real buttons, and the console never appears at all.
• Drag and drop. Drop a video onto the app, or click to browse for one. Your videos
  no longer have to sit in the same folder as the tool.
• Cancel button. Long compressions and downloads can be stopped mid-run, which was
  not possible before.
• The window stays responsive while it works, so you can move it and watch live
  progress instead of staring at a frozen screen.
• Nothing to install. ffmpeg, ffprobe and yt-dlp now come bundled in the download,
  so there is no winget setup and nothing to add to your PATH.
• NVIDIA GPU mode is now a simple on/off switch, shown only if you have an NVIDIA
  card, and each preset tells you the encoder settings it will use.
• YouTube MP4 lists only the qualities your video actually offers, and downloads
  above 1080p are merged correctly.
• Clearer mistakes. Bad timestamps and unreadable files are explained on screen
  instead of scrolling past in a console.
• Settings shows which ffmpeg/ffprobe/yt-dlp the app is actually using, which makes
  "it suddenly stopped working" far easier to diagnose.
