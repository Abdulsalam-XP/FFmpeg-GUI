What's New in This Update:

Version 3.0.1 - a polish pass over the new editor: undo covers more, exports of
deep zooms are twice as fast, and "done" finally looks like done.

Improved:

- "Done" messages are unmissable now: when a compression, merge, download or
  save finishes, the confirmation is big, bold, bright green and glowing -
  no more squinting to check whether it worked. Saving with Ctrl+S gets the
  same treatment (it used to be plain grey text).
- Exporting a video with a deep zoom glide is about 2x faster. The encoder
  now only processes the part of the frame your zoom can actually show,
  instead of blowing the whole frame up first.
- Undo now covers fades: turning a dissolve on or off, or changing its
  length, is a proper undo step, and Ctrl+Z restores your fades exactly.
- The main video track's header wears a gold edge, so V1 reads as "the"
  track at a glance next to overlay tracks.

Fixed:

- Right after splitting a clip, the "+ FADE" button at the new cut was
  unclickable (the playhead sitting exactly there swallowed the click).
- A caption timed to the incoming side of a crossfade did not appear during
  the dissolve - it either showed up late or not at all. It now fades in
  with its footage, mid-dissolve included.
- A zoom keyframe at the very start of the timeline drew as a clipped
  half-diamond.
- The zoom badge could read "2,5x" instead of "2.5x" on systems using a
  comma decimal.
- A hand-edited or corrupted project file could ask the export for a
  zero-size picture-in-picture; it is now clamped to something sane on load.
