What's New in This Update:

Version 2.1 - keep ffmpeg and yt-dlp up to date from inside the app, plus two
fixes worth updating for on their own.

• Settings now shows the version of ffmpeg, ffprobe and yt-dlp you actually have,
  and offers an Update button when a newer build is available. The download runs
  in the background with progress, and can be cancelled. Nothing to fetch by hand.
• Keeping yt-dlp current is the single best fix for "YouTube links stopped
  working" - YouTube changes things often, and an out-of-date yt-dlp is nearly
  always the reason.
• The check is remembered for an hour, so reopening Settings is instant instead of
  going to the network every time.
• The video you pick is now shown as a card with a thumbnail from the clip itself,
  plus resolution, frame rate, length and size - instead of a faint line of grey
  text above the drop area.
• The GPU switch and the preset highlight now move instead of snapping, and the
  card that spots your graphics card says so plainly.
• New icons for Trim, Merge Audio and Settings, and the two YouTube screens no
  longer share the same one.

Fixed:

• Cancel stopped working after the first job. Starting a second compression,
  merge, trim or download in the same session left a Cancel button that quietly
  did nothing, and closing the app was the only way to stop the job. It now works
  every time.
• Clicking Compress (or Merge, Trim, Download) while a job was already running
  started a second job on top of it. The progress bar jumped back and forth
  between the two, and both wrote to the same output file at once. The button is
  now disabled until the running job finishes or is cancelled.
